## 2. What we do (products and services)

Dynotech operates a **cloud communications (CPaaS / cloud-telephony) SaaS platform**. Customers are
businesses and individuals who buy and manage virtual phone services online, with no hardware. The
platform offers:

- **Virtual phone numbers** — monthly rental of local/toll-free numbers (primarily [US]; also
  [other countries]).
- **Cloud IVR / auto-attendant** — menu-driven call handling ("press 1 for sales…").
- **SIP softphone** — make and receive calls from the app / a softphone client.
- **Call forwarding, voicemail and voicemail-to-text.**
- **AI receptionist and AI voice agents** — automated answering and call handling.
- **Business SMS and bulk SMS campaigns** — sending and receiving text messages.
- **eSIM / mobile data plans** — resold mobile connectivity for travellers and remote teams.
- **Add-ons** — extra lines, call recording, custom caller ID, higher-tier plans.

All services are delivered digitally and consumed online. There are no physical goods shipped.

## 3. How we make a profit (revenue model)

The business runs on a **prepaid wallet model** combined with **subscriptions**:

1. **Number rental (recurring):** customers pay a monthly fee per phone number / plan.
2. **Plan subscriptions (recurring):** tiered plans (e.g. Business tier) unlock features such as
   scheduling, campaigns and AI agents.
3. **Usage (prepaid, pay-as-you-go):** customers **top up a wallet balance**, then calls, SMS and
   other usage are deducted from that balance at per-minute / per-message rates.
4. **Add-ons and one-off services:** extra lines, recording, number replacement, eSIM data bundles.

**Margin** comes from the spread between the wholesale rates we pay our telecom carriers and
the retail rates we charge customers, plus recurring subscription and rental fees.

### Role of Mollie in this model
Mollie is used **only to top up the prepaid wallet** (and to pay subscription/rental invoices that
draw from it). The flow is:

1. Customer chooses a top-up amount on cloudivr.net.
2. Customer pays by **card / PayPal / local methods** through Mollie (charged in EUR/USD).
3. On Mollie's "paid" confirmation, the equivalent **USD is credited to the customer's wallet**.
4. The customer later spends that balance on services.

Because funds are **prepaid and credited to a balance the customer controls**, there is **no
delivery delay and very low dispute/chargeback exposure** — the customer is buying account credit,
not an undelivered future good.

## 6. Risk management, compliance and refunds

Dynotech takes telecom abuse and fraud prevention seriously and operates active controls:

- **Customer onboarding / KYC** — accounts and number purchases are verified; sensitive numbers
  require registration.
- **Anti-spam / anti-scam monitoring** — automated AI + rule-based screening of outbound SMS and
  calls (including bulk campaigns) for phishing, impersonation and fraud, with staff review and the
  ability to hold or block suspicious activity.
- **Usage limits and velocity controls** on calling and messaging to prevent abuse.
- **Prepaid model** — customers cannot run up unpaid balances; services stop when the wallet is
  empty, which also protects against payment default.
- **Refund policy** — wallet top-ups are account credit; refunds of unused balance are handled
  per our published terms at [terms URL].
- **Chargeback handling** — disputes are reconciled against the prepaid wallet and usage records;
  because value is prepaid and auditable, disputes are rare.
