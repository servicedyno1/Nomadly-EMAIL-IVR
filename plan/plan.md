# CloudIVR — Landing Redesign, Product Visuals & App-Wide UI/UX Fixes

A redesign of the CloudIVR public landing page into a clean, light, trustworthy product site with realistic product visuals, alongside an app-wide UI/UX audit delivered as ranked findings. Colours move to a calm light palette with an indigo/violet accent, and the highest-impact fixes from the audit are applied to the main signed-in screens.

## Who it's for
- Prospective CloudIVR customers evaluating a virtual-number / IVR / cloud-phone service on cloudivr.net.
- Existing users who sign in and run numbers, menus, voicemail and campaigns day to day.
- The product owner, who wants a credible, conversion-ready public page and a more consistent, easier-to-use app.

## Core features and experience
- **App-wide UI/UX audit, presented first as a ranked list of findings** (most to least severe), grouped by four lenses: Hick's Law (too many choices at once), Gestalt (grouping, alignment, visual hierarchy), the 8-point spacing grid (consistent spacing and sizing), and the product's core loops (sign up → buy a number → set it up → use it).
- **Landing page rebuilt in a clean, light visual system** — generous whitespace, a clear typographic hierarchy, consistent spacing, and an indigo/violet accent replacing the current neon green.
- **Realistic product visuals** — polished mock-ups of the dashboard and call/IVR screens shown in the hero and through the page, so it reads as a real, shipping product.
- **Same substance, re-styled and tightened** — hero with one clear value proposition and a single primary call to action, coverage, the live free test call, features, how-it-works, transparent pricing (from the live catalogue), FAQ, and a closing call to action.
- **Top audit fixes applied to the signed-in app now** — one consistent colour and spacing system, clearer navigation and grouping, and fewer competing choices on the main task screens.
- Everything responsive on desktop and mobile; no existing features, pricing or content removed.

## User flow
- **Landing:** visitor arrives → hero states what CloudIVR is and offers one primary action (get a number) with a secondary one (try a free call) → scannable, grouped sections for coverage and features → realistic product visuals build trust → transparent pricing → FAQ answers common objections → closing call to action to sign up. Each step limits competing choices.
- **Signed-in app:** after the fixes, the core path (sign up → buy a number → set it up → use it) has clearer steps, consistent spacing and grouping, and less on-screen clutter at each decision point.

## UI/UX feel
- Clean, light, trustworthy SaaS — calm and credible rather than flashy.
- Indigo/violet as the single accent for primary actions and highlights; neutral greys for structure; the green parrot logo kept as the brand mark.
- Consistent 8-point spacing, a strong but restrained type hierarchy, clear grouping (Gestalt), and subtle, tasteful motion.
- The signed-in app keeps its darker working surface but adopts the same indigo/violet accent and spacing rules so the two feel like one product.

## Implementation phases

**Phase 1 — built now (MVP)**
1. App-wide UI/UX audit delivered as a ranked list of findings across the public and signed-in screens.
2. Full landing-page redesign: light palette, indigo/violet accent, refreshed typography, 8-point spacing, clearer hierarchy, and realistic product visuals — keeping all current content, pricing and the live demo call, responsive on desktop and mobile.
3. Highest-impact audit fixes applied to the core signed-in screens (shared navigation/shell, dashboard, buy-a-number, number settings): consistent colour and spacing, clearer grouping, and reduced choice overload.

**Phase 2 — later**
Extend the fixes to the remaining signed-in screens (campaigns, inbox, wallet, audio, logs and the rest), with consistent empty, loading and error states.

**Phase 3 — later**
Accessibility pass (colour contrast, focus and keyboard states), micro-interactions, and consolidation of the design into reusable style tokens for lasting consistency.

## Assumptions
- Target is the CloudIVR site (cloudivr.net). The name "CloudIVR" and the green parrot logo are kept; only the UI accent changes to indigo/violet — the logo itself is not recoloured.
- The public landing becomes light; the signed-in app keeps its dark working surface but aligns to the new accent and spacing (it is not re-themed to light in this round).
- Realistic product visuals are newly produced mock-ups based on the real screens; they illustrate the product rather than being live screenshots.
- Pricing, coverage and feature copy stay accurate to what the product offers today; the free test-call demo and catalogue-driven pricing stay functional.
- The audit is delivered as findings; in this round only the landing and the top core-loop screens are changed — everything else is deferred to later phases.
- No new third-party integrations, no data or pricing changes, and no removal of existing features or sections.
- The landing keeps its current set of sections, restyled and tightened, rather than being replaced with entirely different sections.
